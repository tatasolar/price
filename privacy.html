Privacy and data protection

Privacy Notice

Effective: November 2026

This Privacy Notice describes how Rooftop Solar Guide collects, uses, protects and retains information submitted through public pages, calculators, consultation forms, guide requests and restricted staff accounts.

1. Information collected

  • Consultation information: name, email address, phone number, state, optional system capacity and message.
  • Guide requests: email address and request timestamps.
  • Staff accounts: account email, password hash, role and session information.
  • Security information: limited server and request logs needed to prevent abuse and diagnose failures.

2. How information is used

Submitted information is used for customer follow-up, administrative review, service security and recordkeeping. Calculator values provide estimates and are not presented as binding quotations.

3. Database storage and access

Request data is stored in the configured MySQL database. Access is restricted to the application account and authorized operators. Passwords are stored as cryptographic hashes, never as plain text. Database credentials and .env configuration must remain outside public web folders and repository uploads.

4. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only while needed for the inquiry, recordkeeping, dispute resolution or applicable legal requirements. You may request correction or deletion of information associated with a submitted request.

5. Cookies and sessions

An essential HTTP-only session cookie supports staff sign-in. Public forms do not require a login cookie. Staff sessions, form validation and CSRF controls are designed to limit unauthorized access.

6. Analytics and external applications

This implementation does not include third-party advertising or analytics. WhatsApp, social networks and external official sites use their own privacy practices. Opening an external application does not transfer form or database data to that application.

7. Sharing and disclosures

Submitted request details are not sold or shared for unrelated marketing. Information may be disclosed to authorized staff or service providers needed to operate the application, or where disclosure is legally required.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information and ask how a submitted inquiry was handled.

9. Children and sensitive information

The planning tools are intended for adults or authorized representatives. Do not submit sensitive financial, identity or medical information unless it is genuinely needed and requested.

10. Changes to this notice

The effective date identifies the latest publication. Material changes should be reviewed before they are made live.

11. Contact and privacy requests

Use the contact page or WhatsApp support for privacy, correction or deletion requests.

This notice is a practical summary, not legal advice. The site operator should have it reviewed for local legal requirements before production use.
