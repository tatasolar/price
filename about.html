About the guide

Clear information for better solar planning

This site brings rooftop solar costs, system sizing, subsidy basics and installation considerations into one practical guide.

Educational, not a quotation

Prices, production estimates and payback examples are general planning references. A real project depends on the roof, local electricity tariff, equipment, installer, DISCOM requirements and current policy.

This guide is not an official government portal and does not represent or promise services from a particular installer, manufacturer or training provider.

How to use the information

  • Compare the assumptions behind estimates before comparing headline prices.
  • Confirm current subsidy eligibility and procedure on the PM Surya Ghar portal.
  • Ask a qualified local professional to assess structural and electrical requirements.
